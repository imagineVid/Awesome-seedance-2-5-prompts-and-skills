## What is Seedance 2.5?

**Seedance 2.5** is the name being used for the next Seedance video-model release disclosed in July 2026 reporting. ByteDance's public Seed model catalog does not yet provide a complete Seedance 2.5 model card, stable API identifier, or detailed public specification. This repository therefore separates confirmed first-party material from community claims and will update its scope when ByteDance publishes durable documentation.

The prompt library focuses on what can be inspected in a real result: reference use, visible action, camera path, beat timing, dialogue and sound cues, continuity constraints, and editing intent. Numeric capability claims remain out of the collection until they can be tied to a stable first-party source.

- **Assign a job to every reference** - State which input controls identity, composition, movement, sound, or visual treatment
- **Describe visible cause and effect** - Connect each action to the motion, reaction, or environmental change the viewer should see
- **Block the scene in beats** - Break a sequence into observable moments instead of summarizing the story
- **Direct the sound track** - Specify dialogue, Foley, ambience, and music only where each cue advances the scene
- **Name the camera path** - Define framing, movement, subject distance, and the transition between important shots
- **Write continuity as a constraint** - Protect identity, wardrobe, product geometry, spatial layout, and lighting across the sequence

**Status references:** [Seedance 2.5 release reporting](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/bytedance-unveils-seedance-2-5-video-model) · [ByteDance Seed model catalog](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/models) · [Available Seedance workflow on ImagineVid](https://imaginevid.io/seedance-2-0)

### Turn a Prompt into a Production Template

A reusable Seedance prompt keeps scene variables separate from direction. Replace the subject, location, spoken line, or product while retaining the camera path, beat structure, sound cues, reference roles, and continuity rules that made the test informative.

**Template pattern:**
```
[FORMAT]. References: [ASSET -> PURPOSE]. Scene: [SUBJECT + ACTION + SETTING]. Camera: [FRAMING + PATH]. Beats: [ORDERED EVENTS]. Audio: [DIALOGUE + FOLEY + AMBIENCE]. Preserve: [IDENTITY / OBJECT / SPACE]. Avoid: [VISIBLE FAILURE MODES].
```

Start with one visible action and one camera decision. Add references, sound, timing, and preservation constraints only when they solve a concrete production problem, then change one variable per test.

00:03-00:06 | Close-Quarters Blitz & Facial Impact

[Visual Effects]: High-frequency "impact frames" (staccato light and shadow flashes) occur the instant the face is struck; sweat appears as glowing particles under side lighting.

00:06-00:10 | Pursuit and Liver-Targeting Knee Strike
[Action]: @ Image2 denies the opponent a chance to recover, stepping in to close the distance; the right hand hooks the back of @ Image1's neck and pulls downward while the right knee drives violently upward, delivering a knee strike to the liver. @ Image1 retches, body arching and curling in pain.

[Visual Effects]: On impact, localized cracks appear on the ground and a dense cloud of dust and grit explodes outward.

00:12-00:14 | Final Struggle
[Action]: @ Image1 lies in the dust, curled in pain; the right hand weakly clutches the abdomen, the chest heaves with labored breathing, and the eyes lose focus.
[Camera Technique]: Slow overhead close-up; the camera rotates slightly and pulls back with a crane move.
